## Workflow
- When troubleshooting server/runtime issues, prefers a clean slate: kill all related processes and restart, rather than continuing incremental diagnosis. Confidence: 0.6
- On incremental aesthetic tweaks to something the user already likes, they delegate the judgment call outright ("do what you think best") — make one confident design decision (evolve the direction without losing its identity) and present the result; no option lists or interviews for refinements of an approved direction. Confidence: 0.6
- Gives terse execution approval (e.g., "Do it") after a plan is presented; proceed immediately once explicitly approved. Confidence: 0.6
